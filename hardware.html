Simple Hardware Architecture Explained

An IoT system consists of Sensors (inputs), a Microcontroller (the brain), and Actuators (outputs). Here is how each physical component works and why it was selected for this project.

🧠
ESP32 / NodeMCU
The Brain (Microcontroller)
3.3V Power

Central controller running the 2-second sensing loop. Reads sensor ADC values, evaluates irrigation thresholds, and handles Wi-Fi telemetry.

Capacitive Moisture v1.2
The Sensor (Substrate Input)
GPIO 34 (ADC)

Corrosion-resistant probe measuring soil dielectric permittivity. Outputs an analog voltage (1.2V–3.0V) directly to GPIO 34.

Analog Signal Output: 1.82 V
⚡
5V Relay Module
The Actuator Switch (High-Power Drive)
GPIO 23 / D1

Optocoupler-isolated electronic switch that safely turns the 5V DC submersible pump on and off without overloading the microcontroller.

RELAY: OFF
